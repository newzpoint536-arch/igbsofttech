Frequently asked questions

What platforms do you specialize in as a Meta Ads agency Nigeria?

We focus on Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok Ads — the top converting channels for Nigerian audiences. We also advise on LinkedIn for B2B.

How do you reduce customer acquisition cost?

Through high intent audience layering (lookalikes, engaged custom audiences), creative that prequalifies, and constant bid/pacing optimization. We also plug funnel leaks.

Do you manage the ad spend or do we?

You retain full control of the ad account & billing. We manage strategy, creatives, and optimization. Some clients prefer us to have billing access for speed — we're transparent either way.

What results can I expect in the first 30 days?

We stabilize tracking, kill wasted spend, and usually see 20‑30% improvement in CPA. Full funnel scaling takes 60‑90 days.
