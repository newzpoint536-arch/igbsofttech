About IGB SOFT-TECH

Leading digital transformation for Nigerian businesses since 2020

Our Mission

To empower Nigerian businesses with innovative digital solutions that drive growth, enhance visibility, and maximize return on investment through data-driven strategies and cutting-edge technology.

Our Vision

To become Nigeria's most trusted digital marketing partner, recognized for transforming businesses and setting new standards in digital excellence across Africa.

Our Story

Founded in 2020, IGB SOFT-TECH emerged from a simple observation: Nigerian businesses possessed immense potential but often lacked the digital expertise to compete effectively.

Our founder, George Iwolo, recognized this gap and assembled a team of digital experts passionate about leveraging technology to solve business challenges. Starting with small local businesses in Asaba, we've grown to serve clients across Nigeria.

Today, we combine local market expertise with global best practices to deliver results that matter.
